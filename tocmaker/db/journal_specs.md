## 二、通用设计原则（出版社原话）

### ACS —— "不透露具体结果"的 TOC 图
来源：[ACS Guidelines for Table of Contents/Abstract Graphics（2024-02-28）](https://pubsapp.acs.org/paragonplus/submission/toc_abstract_graphics_guidelines.pdf)（一手）
- "This graphic should capture the reader's attention and, in conjunction with the manuscript title, should give the reader a **quick visual impression of the essence of the manuscript without providing specific results**."
- "Text should usually be limited to the labeling of compounds, reaction arrows, and diagrams." 无衬线（Helvetica）8 pt，≥6 pt。
- "The graphic must be entirely original, unpublished artwork created by one of the coauthors." 不得出现任何人（照片/绘画/漫画），不得含邮票、货币、商标（公司 logo、图片、产品）。
- ES&T 补充（[revisions 页](https://pubs.acs.org/page/esthag/submission/revisions.html)）："This image, acting as a 'visual abstract' should simply illustrate your manuscript **without being an exact duplicate of contained figures**."
- Chem. Res. Toxicol. 补充：表格与谱图不可作为 TOC 图；复杂结构式与细小细节不鼓励；无图注。

### Elsevier —— "展示主要发现"，有起点与终点
来源：[Elsevier Graphical abstract 页](https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/tools-and-resources/graphical-abstract)（一手）；[MCP 期刊 GA 指南](https://legacyfileshare.elsevier.com/promis_misc/MCP-GA-Guidelines-Examples.pdf)；[Elsevier Connect](https://www.elsevier.com/connect/using-graphical-abstracts-to-enrich-and-expand-the-reach-of-your-research)
- "A graphical abstract is a single, concise, pictorial and visual summary of the **main findings** of the article."（与 ACS 相反：Elsevier 要把关键结论画出来）
- "The graphical abstract should have a **clear start and end**, preferably 'reading' from **top to bottom or left to right**."
- "No additional text, outline or synopsis should be included. Any text or label must be part of the image file. Please do not use unnecessary white space or a heading 'graphical abstract' within the image file."
- MCP 指南："highlight **one process** or make **one point** clear"；标签简单、文字最少；布局可为线性（上下/左右）或中心放射式（箭头向外）。
- Elsevier Connect：不要直接搬用正文图；"less is more"，留足留白；结构 = 研究问题（起）→ 如何解决（中）→ 结论/"so what"（终）。
- 技术：最小 1328×531 px @300 dpi；Times/Arial/Courier/Symbol；第三方素材须授权；生成式 AI 须符合 Elsevier 政策（部分刊明令禁止）。

### Cell Press —— 一幅图、一句话、零数据
来源：[Cell Press Graphical Abstract Guidelines PDF](https://www.cell.com/pb/assets/raw/shared/figureguidelines/GA_guide.pdf)；[CrossTalk 博客](https://crosstalk.cell.com/blog/attract-readers-at-a-glance-with-your-graphical-abstract)；[iScience 图规范](https://www.cell.com/iscience/figureguidelines)（一手）
- "The graphical abstract is **one single-panel image** designed to give readers an immediate understanding of the **take-home message** of the paper."
- "**Not include data items of any type**; all content should be in a graphical form."
- "The graphical abstract should be **distinct from any model figures** or diagrams in the paper itself."
- 有明确起止，自上而下或自左向右；强调本文新发现，不堆砌既往文献细节。
- 颜色用于引导注意；避免高饱和原色；"when in doubt, try to avoid **red/green and red/black** as comparative color choices."
- 1200 px 见方 @300 dpi；Arial/Avenir 12–16 pt；TIFF/PDF/JPEG；文件只含图本身（勿贴在大白底上）；通用生成式 AI 工具不可用（BioRender 等专用工具可）。

### Wiley —— TOC 图 + 短摘要文字
来源：[Wiley Guidelines for Graphic in Table of Contents PDF](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/pb-assets/25673165/Graphic%20TOC-1577161100207.pdf)；[Wiley GA 指南 PDF](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/pb-assets/Graphical%20Abstract%20PDF-1683501909.pdf)；[Wiley TOC 工作流文档](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/pb-assets/Martin%20G/Graphical%20Abstracts-1546616810660.pdf)；[Advanced 系列指南](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/author-guidelines)（一手）
- TOC 图应 "simple but informative"；颜色有助则用；避免长文字；可为结构、曲线、绘图、照片或方案，文字通常限于化合物标注、箭头与示意。
- GA 指南：图须自成一体、文字最少、**白底**、无衬线 12–16 pt、高分辨率标准格式；呈现文章主要发现并自解释。
- TOC 条目 = 图 + **短摘要文字**（名义上限 100 词）；网页上图被缩至 180 px 宽，细节须耐缩放。
- Advanced 系列（Advanced Science 等）：ToC 文字 **50–60 词**，第三人称、面向泛读者；图 55×50 mm 或 110×20 mm；标注 ≥10 pt；作者须拥有全部元素版权。
- 部分 Wiley 刊：禁止 AI 平台生成的图；人物形象避免刻板印象；避免宗教/政治意象。

### RSC —— 图 + 1–2 句"不是图注"的文字
来源：[RSC Advances](https://www.rsc.org/publishing/publish-with-us/publish-a-journal-article/rsc-advances)、[Soft Matter](https://www.rsc.org/publishing/publish-with-us/publish-a-journal-article/soft-matter)、[ES: Nano](https://www.rsc.org/publishing/publish-with-us/publish-a-journal-article/environmental-science-nano) 作者指南（一手）
- TOC 条目 = 吸睛的图（最大 8×4 cm，用满空间，彩色）+ 1–2 句概括主要发现。
- "The text should **not be a description or caption** of the graphical abstract image."
- 图 "simple, but informative"；"complex schematics or spectra should be avoided."

### MDPI
来源：[Toxics 投稿须知](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/toxics/instructions)（一手）
- GA 不得与正文任一图相同、不得是若干子图的简单叠放、不得是"摘要文字 + 一张图"。
- 避免大段文字与大片空白；图内不写 "Graphical Abstract" 标题；原创，无邮票/货币/商标/受版权素材。
